# 901 — Obsidian Precision Art Direction

**Status:** Proposed  
**Audience:** Product design, UI engineering, desktop-shell, QA, and coding agents  
**Canonical:** Yes

---

## 1. Character

Obsidian Precision combines the quiet hierarchy of modern productivity tools with the spatial clarity of professional production software.

It should feel:

- intentional;
- precise;
- native to desktop;
- technically capable;
- premium without visual noise;
- modern without relying on generic “AI gradients.”

## 2. What It Is Not

- not a recolored SaaS dashboard;
- not a black version of the former web Mirae;
- not a cyberpunk gaming interface;
- not a glassmorphism concept with low legibility;
- not an OBS clone;
- not a chat application stretched across the whole product.

## 3. Brand Continuity

The previous Mirae symbol is retained because it is compact, geometric, monochrome, and readable at small sizes.

The prior light product language is not retained. Only these principles survive:

- exact spacing;
- monochrome-first identity;
- restrained accent;
- careful motion;
- Mirae-owned components;
- high screenshot quality.

## 4. Visual Signature

- near-black canvas;
- cool neutral panel stack;
- periwinkle electric accent;
- thin borders with low contrast;
- compact typography;
- small radii for controls;
- larger radii for floating surfaces;
- subtle blue/purple atmospheric light only in previews or hero surfaces;
- the white Mirae mark in the titlebar and app icon.
