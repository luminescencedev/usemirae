# 902 — Product Experience Principles

**Status:** Proposed  
**Audience:** Product design, UI engineering, desktop-shell, QA, and coding agents  
**Canonical:** Yes

---

## Principles

### Canvas first

The content being produced is the star. Sidebars recede until needed.

### Dense, not cramped

Mirae should expose real production power without forcing users through large dashboard cards. Density is controlled through typography, grouping, and progressive disclosure.

### Operational certainty

A user must know at a glance whether Mirae is idle, previewing, recording, live, reconnecting, degraded, or failed.

### Spatial continuity

Selections, panels, and moved items keep spatial continuity through restrained motion.

### Keyboard-native

Power users can operate scenes, sources, properties, outputs, and the command palette without a pointer.

### Calm failure

Failures explain impact, what continues to work, automatic recovery, and next action.

### Advanced by progressive disclosure

Common controls are immediately available. Rare technical settings live in expandable advanced groups.

### AI as a layer, not the product

Future AI assistance belongs in the command palette, contextual actions, and inspectable operations. It never replaces the production workspace.
